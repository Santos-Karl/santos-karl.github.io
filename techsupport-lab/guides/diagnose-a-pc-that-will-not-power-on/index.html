Diagnose a PC that will not power on

Safe first checks for a desktop that shows no lights, no fans, and no display, without opening the power supply.

Overview

Start with the simplest causes: the wall outlet, the power cable, and the power switch on the back of the power supply. Work through the list before opening the case, and never open or disassemble the power supply unit.

Symptoms

  • Pressing the power button produces no fans, lights, or beeps.
  • The case lights come on briefly and then turn off.
  • The PC starts but the display stays dark (see the no-display guide).

Possible causes

  • Wall outlet, surge protector, or cable faulty.
  • Power supply switch set to off (O position).
  • Front panel power button or its cable disconnected.
  • Power supply failure, or a short from a loose part.

Requirements

  • Working outlet and a known-good power cable.
  • Screwdriver for case access, only if the earlier checks fail.
  • Anti-static wrist strap or touching bare metal of the case before handling parts.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Unplug the PC, then plug it into a different, known-working outlet, bypassing any surge protector.
  2. Check the power switch on the back of the power supply is set to I (on).
  3. Swap the power cable with a known-good one.
  4. Plug the PC in and press the power button while watching for fan spin or lights.
  5. If nothing happens, check the front panel power button cable is seated on the motherboard header.
  6. Only if the above fails: with the PC unplugged, check the motherboard's 24-pin and CPU 8-pin power connectors are fully seated.

Troubleshooting notes

  • A power supply that powers the fans but not the rest of the PC is a separate issue; test with a PSU tester or a replacement, not by opening the unit.

Common mistakes

  • Pressing the power button repeatedly while the cable is loose.
  • Working inside the case while it is still plugged in.

Security and safety warnings

  • Never open or disassemble a power supply unit. Capacitors can hold a dangerous charge even when unplugged.
  • Unplug the PC before touching any internal part.
  • Use an anti-static strap or touch the case metal to discharge static before handling components.

Verification steps

  • The fans spin and the motherboard status light (if any) turns on after pressing the power button.
  • The PC reaches the BIOS or display output.
