Component Preparation

Confirm every part is present, undamaged, and compatible before anything is installed.

Loading progress...

Required tools and materials

  • Parts list or order sheet
  • Motherboard, CPU, RAM, and GPU manuals
  • Anti-static wrist strap
  • Clean, hard, non-carpeted work surface
  • Good light and a small container for screws

Step-by-step procedure

Tick each step when you finish it. Your ticks are saved in this browser.

Important precautions

  • Keep every part in its anti-static bag until you are ready to install it.
  • Do not open the power supply. Check its label and rating only.
  • Do not work on carpet, and avoid clothing that causes static.

Expected result

Every part is present and matches the others. You have a written list of any missing items or compatibility problems to resolve before assembly.

Common problems and solutions

  • A part is missing or damaged.

    Record it on the checklist with a photo, and contact the supplier before starting assembly.

  • The CPU socket does not match the board.

    Stop. Do not force the part. Return it or exchange it for a compatible part.

  • The GPU or cooler might not fit the case.

    Measure the part and compare it with the case's stated maximum before installing.
