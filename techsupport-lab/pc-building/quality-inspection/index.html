Task 07 of 11

Quality Inspection

Inspect the finished PC against the order, confirm the specifications, and resolve any issues before packing.

Loading progress...

Required tools and materials

  • Order sheet and specification sheet
  • Flashlight
  • Screwdriver for checking screws
  • Test results from the previous step

Step-by-step procedure

Tick each step when you finish it. Your ticks are saved in this browser.

Important precautions

  • Do not sign off an inspection with unresolved issues. Record them as open items instead.
  • Note what changed each time you repeat a repair.

Expected result

The PC matches the order, every check passes, and any remaining issue is recorded with a plan.

Common problems and solutions

  • A part does not match the order.

    Record the difference, and tell your manager before the PC moves on.

  • A rattle inside the case.

    Look for a loose screw, cable, or standoff. Tighten or secure it, then check again.
